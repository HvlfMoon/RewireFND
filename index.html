The Most Common Neurological Disorder You've Never Heard Of

Functional Neurological Disorder (FND) is a condition where the brain and nervous system have difficulty sending and receiving signals correctly. It is not rare either. It is a common cause of neurological disability, representing up to one-third of outpatient neurology clinic visits and the second most common reason for a neurology consultation. The estimated minimum prevalence is 80–140 per 100,000 people, though it is frequently underdiagnosed.
